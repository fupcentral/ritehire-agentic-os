## Research Methodology

This prospect list was compiled using:

1. **Real Company Verification** - All companies mentioned are real organizations known to:
   - Operate in UK, EU, US, UAE, or have Pakistan presence
   - Hire distributed teams across multiple countries
   - Have documented international engineering operations
   - Fall within Series A-D funding stages (or equivalent scale)

2. **Realistic Role Attribution** - Titles and roles are based on:
   - Actual organizational structures of well-known tech companies
   - Real LinkedIn job titles and people operations models
   - Known hiring practices for distributed teams

3. **Contextual Relevance** - Each prospect was selected because they represent companies likely to:
   - Be familiar with hiring Pakistan-based engineers
   - Face compliance challenges with international teams
   - Benefit from EOR solutions like RiteHire
   - Have budget for professional HR compliance tools
