## MONITORING & OPTIMIZATION

**Track Metrics:**
- Impressions & engagement rate (target: 3-5% min)
- Click-through rate (target: 0.5-1% min)
- Saves (quality signal)
- Comments (engagement depth)
- Shares (reach multiplier)
- Profile visits & DMs

**High-Performing Content (Typical for B2B SaaS/EOR):**
- EOR Education (compliance facts): 4-6% engagement
- Market Insight (salary data): 3-5% engagement
- Social Proof (speed metrics): 2-4% engagement
- Thought Leadership (talent trends): 2-3% engagement

**A/B Testing Ideas:**
- Headline variants (question vs. statement)
- Stat callouts (leading with % vs. $ savings)
- Visual style (minimal cards vs. full-bleed charts)
