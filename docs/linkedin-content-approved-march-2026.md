The 2026 playbook for building a distributed team in Pakistan:

**City selection:**  
- Karachi: fintech, banking tech, enterprise software  
- Lahore: product, full-stack, mobile  
- Islamabad: defence tech, government tech, consultancy  

Most companies default to Lahore for early engineering hires. That's where the largest concentration of product-focused engineers is right now.

**Role sequencing (first 6 months):**  
Month 1–2: Senior engineer (existing architecture context — hire someone who can work independently)  
Month 3–4: QA or junior engineer (now you have enough process to onboard someone junior)  
Month 5–6: Second senior or specialist (by now you know what gap you actually have)

Do not hire junior-first. The support overhead before you have process will burn the senior engineer who has to carry it.

**Employment structure:**  
Every hire should be an employee from day one. Not a contractor. Not a trial.

If you're not ready to make someone an employee, you're not ready to hire in Pakistan.

**Common mistakes in month 1:**  
→ No local HR point of contact (engineers need someone to call when there's a payroll question)  
→ Team communication across too many time zones (Pakistan is UTC+5 — works well with UK, manageable with US East Coast)  
→ Annual leave policy not written for Pakistan calendar (Eid al-Fitr + Eid al-Adha require advance planning)

**What works:**  
→ Monthly 1:1 cadence between founder and Pakistan team lead (visibility without micromanagement)  
→ Statutory benefits handled from day one (EOBI + ESSI + gratuity — signal of intent, not just compliance)  
→ 6-month review with compensation adjustment conversation built into the contract
