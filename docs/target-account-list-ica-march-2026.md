## Account Research Prompts

For each Tier 1 account, run this research before connecting:

1. **Coverage check:** Search "[company name] Pakistan" on LinkedIn and their website. Do they list Pakistan? What do they say about it?
2. **Customer complaints:** Search "[company name] Pakistan" on Twitter/X and G2/Capterra. Any coverage quality complaints?
3. **Team signal:** Does their LinkedIn show Pakistan-based employees? If yes — they're already trying to cover it.
4. **Partner page:** Do they have a partner program page? What does it say about local compliance partners?
5. **Recent funding:** If Series A/B in last 12 months — they're likely expanding coverage to win enterprise clients.

Use these findings to personalise the outreach angle from the CRO strategy template.
